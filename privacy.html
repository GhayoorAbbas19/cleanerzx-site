Privacy Policy — CleanerZX: Phone Junk Cleaner

Developer: ZXApps · Effective date: 29 September 2026 · Last updated: 16 October 2026

CleanerZX: Phone Junk Cleaner (also called "CleanerZX" or "the app"), published on Google Play by ZXApps, is an on-device cleanup tool for photos, videos, contacts, and files. This policy explains what information the app accesses, how it is used, and what leaves your device. Your photos, videos, contacts, and files never leave your device. CleanerZX has no user accounts and no servers of its own. The app is free and supported by advertising served through Google AdMob, and it uses Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics to find crashes and understand which features are used. The sections Advertising and Crash reporting and analytics below describe what that involves.

Summary

  • All scanning and analysis happens locally on your device.
  • Nothing you scan — photos, videos, contacts, or files — ever leaves your phone.
  • ZXApps itself does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data.
  • Ads are shown through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google receives standard advertising data from your device (such as the advertising ID, IP address, and device and app information). Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent first and can change your choice at any time.
  • Crash reports and anonymous usage statistics (which screens and features are used, scan counts and sizes) go to Google Firebase. They never include your photos, contacts, file names, or anything that identifies you personally.
  • The optional "Remove ads" purchase is processed entirely by Google Play. ZXApps never sees your payment details; the app only receives a purchase token from Google Play that confirms the purchase.

Advertising

CleanerZX shows ads from Google AdMob, operated by Google LLC. Ads appear in the following places: a full-screen ad after the welcome screens, an ad card on the Home screen, a rewarded video that you watch to start a Smart scan, and banner ads on the Library and Contacts tabs.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK runs inside the app and, when an ad is requested, sends Google the data needed to select, serve, and measure ads. Depending on your settings this can include:

  • Your device's advertising ID (resettable in Android Settings » Privacy » Ads).
  • IP address and approximate (country/city-level) location derived from it.
  • Device and app information: device model, operating system version, language, screen size, the app's package name and version, and network type.
  • Ad interaction data: which ads were shown, viewed, clicked, or completed.

This data goes to Google, not to ZXApps. ZXApps only sees aggregated reporting (impressions, clicks, and earnings) and cannot identify individual users from it. Google's use of this data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. Your content — photos, videos, contacts, files, scan results — is never shared with Google or any advertiser.

Your choices. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent to personalized advertising before any ad is requested, using Google's consent framework; you can review or change that choice at any time in the app under Account » Privacy options. Everywhere else, you can opt out of ad personalization or reset your advertising ID in Android Settings » Privacy » Ads. The app works fully whether or not you consent to personalized ads.

Crash reporting and analytics

The app uses two Google Firebase services, operated by Google LLC:

  • Firebase Crashlytics sends a report when the app crashes or hits an internal error. A report contains the stack trace, the app version, device model and operating system version, free memory and storage, whether the device is rooted, a random installation identifier (Crashlytics installation ID), and a short log of the app's recent steps (for example "scan_started"). It does not contain your media, contacts, or file names.
  • Firebase Analytics records anonymous usage events: which screens were opened, when the welcome flow was completed, when a scan started and finished and how much space it found, which cleanup categories were used and how many items were removed, how many contacts were deleted, and whether ads were shown or failed to load. Events carry counts and sizes only, never names or content. Analytics also records the app instance ID, device model, OS version, language, country derived from IP address, and app version.

ZXApps uses this data only to fix crashes and improve the app. It is stored by Google on ZXApps' behalf; Crashlytics data is kept for 90 days and Analytics event data for up to 14 months. Google's handling of it is described in the Firebase Privacy and Security page. Crash reporting and analytics cannot be switched off inside the app, but uninstalling the app stops all collection, and the identifiers are reset on reinstall.

Purchases

The app offers a single optional in-app purchase, "Remove ads", which switches advertising off. The purchase is made through Google Play, operated by Google LLC, and is governed by Google Play's terms and privacy policy. Your payment method, billing address, and order history are handled by Google Play and are never transmitted to or stored by ZXApps. The app only receives a purchase token from Google Play, which it uses to confirm that the purchase belongs to the signed-in Google account, acknowledge it, and remember on the device that ads should stay off. The app also records an anonymous analytics event (see below) when a purchase is started, completed, or restored; it contains no payment or account details.

Data collection and sharing

Beyond the advertising, purchase, crash-reporting, and analytics data described above, the app collects no personal or sensitive user data and shares no data with third parties. There is no account or sign-in, and no transfer of your content off the device. The network is used only for ads, the consent form, and Firebase reporting.

Storage and retention

Scan results (for example, which photos were classified as duplicates, and scan history) are stored in a local database on your device only, so results are still there when you reopen the app. This cache is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Your reminder and settings choices are stored the same way.

Deletion and your control

  • Photos and videos you delete through the app are moved to Android's system trash, where they remain recoverable for about 30 days, using Android's own confirmation dialog.
  • Contact deletions are performed only after an explicit in-app confirmation and are permanent.
  • Files deleted by the Junk files tool (in folders you granted) are removed permanently after an explicit confirmation — they do not go to a trash.
  • App uninstalls and cache clearing always go through Android's own system screens; the app never removes another app or its data by itself.
  • You can revoke any permission at any time in Android Settings; the related feature simply stops working.

Security

Because your data never leaves the device, its security is governed by your device's own protections (screen lock, encryption). The app's local database is stored in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read on standard Android devices.

Children

CleanerZX is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children. Ad requests are not tagged as child-directed. If you believe a child has used the app, resetting the device's advertising ID removes the identifier that was shared with Google.
