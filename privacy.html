Privacy Policy — CleanerZX for Android

Developer: ZXApps · Effective date: 29 September 2026

CleanerZX ("the app") is an on-device cleanup tool for photos, videos, contacts, and files. This policy explains what information the app accesses, how it is used, and what leaves your device: nothing does. CleanerZX has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no servers of its own in this release.

Summary

  • All scanning and analysis happens locally on your device.
  • The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.
  • Nothing you scan — photos, videos, contacts, or files — ever leaves your phone.

Data collection and sharing

The app collects no personal or sensitive user data and shares no data with third parties. There are no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs in the app, no account or sign-in, and no data transfer off the device. The app does not use the network to process your content.

Storage and retention

Scan results (for example, which photos were classified as duplicates, and scan history) are stored in a local database on your device only, so results are still there when you reopen the app. This cache is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Your reminder and settings choices are stored the same way.

Deletion and your control

  • Photos and videos you delete through the app are moved to Android's system trash, where they remain recoverable for about 30 days, using Android's own confirmation dialog.
  • Contact deletions are performed only after an explicit in-app confirmation and are permanent.
  • You can revoke any permission at any time in Android Settings; the related feature simply stops working.

Security

Because your data never leaves the device, its security is governed by your device's own protections (screen lock, encryption). The app's local database is stored in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read on standard Android devices.

Children

CleanerZX is not directed at children under 13, and the app collects no data from any user, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes these practices — for example, by adding subscriptions or advertising — this policy will be updated at this URL before that version is released, and the in-app disclosures will be updated to match.
